    Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

    Built from the official trophy descriptions and PowerPyx's launch-week roadmap (difficulty 4/10, about 200 hours, one playthrough, no mission replay, cheats disable trophies and saving). Five stages, in order.

      Roadmap order, trophy by trophy

        Missable trophies

        Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable by PowerPyx, GTABase and the other launch guides — and even that one is unconfirmed. Everything else can be earned after the story in free-roam, because there is no mission replay but Leonida stays open. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots) as insurance.

          Why the rest are safe

          • All 12 story trophies unlock automatically and cannot be missed.
          • Every side-character questline (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) stays available after the story.
          • Strangers & Freaks, robberies, activities and collectibles all stay open in free-roam.
          • Optional missions that disappear do not affect any trophy (PowerPyx).

          Sources & method

          Every guide in this app is written in my own words for this entry. Facts about where, how and when come from the sources below, named beside each trophy's guide — never copied from them. The official names, descriptions and grades are shown as PlayStation publishes them.

            What is still unknown

            Grand Theft Auto VI launched 19 November 2026, a day before this guide was built. One trophy, Bad Luck, I Guess, is still listed as unknown by PowerPyx (believed to relate to the Payday Loans store from the prologue) — its guide says so plainly instead of inventing a method. The Chapter 1 meditation for Positive Mental Attitude is likewise flagged as possibly missable but unconfirmed; the guide tells you to do it anyway, because it costs nothing. Where a fact was genuinely unknowable at build time, the guide says what the official description implies and cites the official description itself.

            Offline

            This app is static files only. It fetches its trophy data from data/trophies.json on this same server and makes no network request at run time. Source links open only if you click them.
